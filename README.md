# 🛡️ HCAI Audit & Governance Platform (`hcai-audit-app`)


An open-source interactive decision-support and regulatory auditing tool that translates Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence (HCAI) literature into actionable engineering and governance workflows.

📌 Overview

Translating high-level AI ethics principles into concrete software specifications and statutory compliance mechanisms remains a critical challenge for developers and policymakers. 

The HCAI Audit Platform operationalizes the findings of our systematic citation-network study (*"Mapping the Human-Centred AI (HCAI) Landscape"*), evaluating AI systems across four core thematic pillars:
1. Ethics & XAI: Explainability (SHAP/LIME), algorithmic fairness, and moral trust calibration.
2. Governance & Accountability: Statutory risk classification under the EU AI Act (Articles 5, 6, 52), audit logging, and Fundamental Rights Impact Assessments (FRIA).
3. Inclusive Design: Value-Sensitive Design (VSD), participatory co-design, and user-override agency.
4. Technical Security & Safety: Operational safety bounds, differential privacy, zero-trust architectures, and generative AI guardrails.

✨ Key Features

 ⚖️ EU AI Act Automated Risk Classifier: Instantly maps system parameters to statutory risk tiers (*Unacceptable, High Risk, GPAI/Transparency, or Minimal Risk).
 📊 20-Indicator Compliance Scorecard: Evaluates qualitative and quantitative parameters across the 4 HCAI clusters to generate a normalized **HCAI Index (0–100%)**.
 🎯 Interactive Radar Visualization: Renders an immediate multi-axis visual profile identifying critical governance gaps prior to deployment.
 💾 Downloadable Audit Reports: Exports structured JSON diagnostic logs for regulatory filing evidence and institutional archives.
